# atg_kc705_mig

RTL: `boards/kc705/lib/atg_kc705_mig.v`.  Parent: atg_kc705_raster.  Implemented in: p012.

## 1. Purpose and place

The KC705 memory: MIG 7 Series DDR3 (1 GB, 64 bit, AXI4 512 bit, ui_clk 200 MHz, regenerated by TCL, design §4.1) behind an AXI4 slave in gclk (the clock crossing to ui_clk is inside: MIG's AXI clock converter or an AXI clock converter IP, chosen in p012).

## 2. Parameters

None.
