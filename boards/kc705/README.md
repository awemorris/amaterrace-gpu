KC705 (XC7K325T-2FFG900)
========================

The KC705's tops come with WS288 p012: MIG DDR3 (regenerated by TCL, not
committed), the MMCM, 64-byte lines and the 512-bit AXI master, the JTAG
register bridge (`am_jtag_regs`), and the display controller.  They are
built with `tools/vivado-build.sh kc705 TOP` (part xc7k325tffg900-2).
The RTL is already linted and simulated in that shape
(`tools/lint.sh`, `sim/tb/atg_top_tb.v`).
